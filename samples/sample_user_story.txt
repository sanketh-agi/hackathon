USER STORY
ID: US-101
As a returning customer,
I want to log in with my email and password,
So that I can access my saved cart and complete my purchase quickly.

Acceptance Criteria:
- I can enter my email and password and click "Log In".
- If my credentials are correct, I am taken to my dashboard with my cart restored.
- If my credentials are wrong, I see a clear error message and can try again.
- If I fail too many times in a row, I am temporarily locked out and told how
  long to wait.

USER STORY
ID: US-102
As a customer with items in my cart,
I want my cart to still be there if I come back later,
So that I don't have to re-add everything I picked out.

Acceptance Criteria:
- If I'm logged in, my cart is saved for at least 30 days.
- If I'm not logged in, I'm reminded that logging in will save my cart.
