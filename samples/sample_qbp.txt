QUARTERLY BUSINESS PROCESS (QBP) REVIEW NOTES
Topic: Checkout & Login Reliability — Q3 Review

Findings discussed:
1. Support ticket volume for "can't log in" rose 22% quarter-over-quarter.
   Root cause analysis points to unclear lockout messaging and customers not
   realizing their cart was lost after being logged out.
2. No current automated regression coverage exists for the account lockout
   flow (5 failed attempts -> 15 minute lock). This was flagged as a risk
   ahead of the holiday traffic surge.
3. Cart persistence behavior differs between logged-in and guest sessions in
   a way that is not clearly documented anywhere customers can see it.
4. Action item: QA to expand automated test coverage to include lockout,
   lockout expiry, and cart persistence across both logged-in and guest
   scenarios before the Q4 traffic increase.
5. Action item: Product to confirm the exact lockout copy and timing with
   Support before next release (see PRD FR-5 through FR-8).

Decision: Prioritize automated end-to-end coverage for login + lockout +
cart persistence as the top QA investment for Q4.
