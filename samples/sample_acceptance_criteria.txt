ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Feature: Login, Lockout, Cart Persistence (ref. PRD v1.0)

AC-1 (Successful login)
Given a registered customer with a valid email and password
When they submit the login form
Then they are redirected to the account dashboard
And their previously saved cart items are restored.

AC-2 (Invalid password)
Given a registered customer enters the correct email but wrong password
When they submit the login form
Then they see "Incorrect email or password"
And the email field retains its value
And the password field is cleared.

AC-3 (Account lockout after repeated failures)
Given a customer has failed to log in 5 times within 15 minutes
When they attempt to log in a 6th time
Then the account is locked for 15 minutes
And the message "Too many failed attempts..." is displayed
And a lockout event is recorded in the audit log.

AC-4 (Lockout expiry)
Given a customer's account was locked 15 minutes ago
When they attempt to log in again with the correct password
Then the login succeeds
And the failed-attempt counter resets to zero.

AC-5 (Guest cart persistence within session)
Given a customer who is not logged in has items in their cart
When they navigate away and return within the same browser session
Then their cart items are still present
And a banner suggests logging in to save the cart long-term.

AC-6 (Payment declined)
Given a customer reaches checkout with a valid cart
When their payment method is declined
Then they see "Your payment was declined..."
And they remain on the checkout page
And their cart is not cleared.
