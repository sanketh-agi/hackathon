BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)
Project: Acme Online Store — Customer Account & Checkout
Version: 1.0

1. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
Acme Corp wants to reduce cart abandonment and support-ticket volume related to
account access and checkout. Customers currently report difficulty logging in
and confusion about order status. The business goal is to increase successful
checkout completion rate by 15% and reduce login-related support tickets by 30%
within one quarter of launch.

2. BACKGROUND
Acme sells home goods through its e-commerce site (https://staging.acme.com).
Customers must have an account to check out. Guest checkout is not offered.
Support data shows the top three complaints are: (a) unclear password
requirements, (b) accounts getting locked with no clear unlock path, and
(c) customers not realizing their cart was emptied after a long idle session.

3. STAKEHOLDERS
- VP of E-Commerce (sponsor)
- Customer Support Lead
- Engineering Lead

4. BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
BR-1: Customers must be able to securely create an account and log in using
      an email address and password.
BR-2: The system must protect customer accounts from brute-force login
      attempts without permanently locking legitimate customers out.
BR-3: Customers must always be able to see the current contents and total of
      their shopping cart before being asked to log in or pay.
BR-4: Customers must receive clear, immediate feedback when something goes
      wrong during login or checkout (wrong password, expired session,
      payment declined), so they do not abandon the purchase out of
      confusion.
BR-5: The business needs an audit trail of failed login attempts and account
      lockouts for fraud and support investigation purposes.

5. SUCCESS METRICS
- Checkout completion rate increases by 15%.
- Login-related support tickets decrease by 30%.
- No increase in fraudulent account creation.
