PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Feature: Login, Account Lockout, and Cart Persistence
Related BRD: Acme Online Store — Customer Account & Checkout (v1.0)
Version: 1.0

1. OVERVIEW
This PRD defines the functional behavior for the login screen, account lockout
policy, and cart persistence on https://staging.acme.com, implementing the
business requirements in the associated BRD.

2. SCOPE
In scope: login form, password rules, lockout mechanism, lockout notification,
cart persistence across sessions, checkout error messaging.
Out of scope: social login (Google/Facebook), guest checkout, multi-factor
authentication (planned for a future release).

3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Login
- FR-1: The login page must present two fields: "Email" and "Password", and a
  "Log In" button.
- FR-2: Email must be validated as a well-formed email address on the client
  side before submission.
- FR-3: On successful login, the customer is redirected to their account
  dashboard and any items previously in their cart (see 3.3) are restored.
- FR-4: On incorrect password, the system displays the message "Incorrect
  email or password" without revealing which field was wrong, and does not
  clear the email field.

3.2 Account Lockout
- FR-5: After 5 consecutive failed login attempts for the same email within a
  15-minute window, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
- FR-6: While locked, the login page displays: "Too many failed attempts.
  Please try again in 15 minutes or reset your password."
- FR-7: A successful login resets the failed-attempt counter to zero.
- FR-8: Every failed attempt and every lockout event is written to the
  security audit log with timestamp, email, and source IP.

3.3 Cart Persistence
- FR-9: Cart contents must persist for a logged-in customer across browser
  sessions for at least 30 days.
- FR-10: For a customer who is not logged in, the cart is stored for the
  duration of the browser session only; a banner reading "Log in to save
  your cart for later" is shown above the cart.
- FR-11: If the cart was modified in another tab/session, the customer sees
  an inline notice "Your cart was updated" before checkout total is
  calculated.

3.4 Checkout Error Handling
- FR-12: If payment is declined, the checkout page displays "Your payment was
  declined. Please check your card details or try a different payment
  method." and keeps the customer on the checkout page with cart intact.
- FR-13: If the session expires mid-checkout, the customer is redirected to
  login with the message "Your session expired. Please log in to continue
  checkout." and the cart is preserved.

4. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (see also separate Acceptance Criteria document)
- Given valid credentials, when the customer logs in, then they land on the
  account dashboard with their prior cart restored.
- Given 5 failed attempts within 15 minutes, when the customer tries a 6th
  time, then the account is locked and the lockout message is shown.
- Given a locked account, when 15 minutes have passed, then the customer can
  log in again successfully with the correct password.
- Given a logged-out customer with items in cart, when they close and reopen
  the browser within the same session, then the cart items are still present.
